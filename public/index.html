📖 Miko Music Quick-Start Guide

Click a section below for quick reference instructions. GitHub README has been similarly mapped.
1. Queue Management & Live Playlist Syncing▼
• Searching & Removing: Use the search bar above the queue to filter songs instantly. Click the ✕ to drop a track.
• Playlist Synchronicity: Every time you queue a playlist URL or shortcode, the bot scrapes YouTube/Spotify in real-time. If you add songs to your actual Spotify playlist, they instantly appear the next time you load it in Miko.
• Non-Destructive Clear: Clicking Clear Queue safely empties the tracks and pauses playback without dropping the bot from voice.
2. The Phantom Dashboard & Auto-Hiding Logs▼
• Persistent Panels: The bot automatically sweeps the Control Panel channel to delete old panels and banners, ensuring your buttons always stay locked at the bottom of the chat.
• Self-Cleaning Replies: "Only you can see this" messages are Discord-side Ephemerals. The bot cannot delete them, but they auto-hide forever when you close the app.
• Now Playing Logs: Enable Auto-Hide Discord Messages to have the bot physically delete song notification logs after 60 seconds.
3. Architecture Note: Music vs Soundboards▼
• Discord physically prevents a single bot from playing two audio streams at the same time.
• For overlapping D&D sound effects, the recommended architecture is deploying a secondary, lightweight sibling bot ("Miko SFX") that hosts an overlapping soundboard without interrupting Miko Music's engine.
