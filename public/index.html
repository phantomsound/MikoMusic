📖 Miko Music Quick-Start Guide

Click a section below for quick reference instructions.
1. Multi-Server & Live Playlist Controls▼
• Multi-Server Support: Use the Active Guild dropdown in the header to switch between Discord servers. The dashboard will instantly isolate and control the audio queue for that specific guild.
• Direct Load: Click ▶ Load next to any playlist in the manager to purge the active queue and start that list without typing or copy-pasting.
• Platform Links: Click 🔗 to jump directly into Spotify or YouTube Music and manage the tracklist in your browser.
2. Discord Controls & Ephemeral Cleanliness▼
• Discord Queue Pagination: Clicking 📋 Queue in Discord now renders interactive pages (|◀, ◀, ▶, ▶|) for full queue navigation.
• Move via Discord: Click ↕️ Move on the Discord panel to open a quick modal and change track orders on the fly.
• Self-Cleaning Messages: All interaction responses are Discord Ephemerals ("Only you can see this") and auto-purge when Discord restarts.
